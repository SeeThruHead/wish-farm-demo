# What it does in YNAB

[← Back](11.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 12 of 12 &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Back to the README →](../../README.md)

Every hour: it sets a target only on the wishes it's funding this month, for exactly what the plan puts in, and clears the rest. When a debt is paid off, it moves the category to Wish Farm Complete and removes the debt's scheduled transaction. It never touches anything else, and it's safe if YNAB is slow or down.
